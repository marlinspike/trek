Some ships, mostly Romulan and Klingon, carry a cloaking device. While it runs, enemy sensors can only find you at very close range.

```console
cloak on
cloak off
decloak
```

- `cloak` and `cloak on` engage it; `cloak off`, `decloak` or `uncloak` drop it.
- The cloak costs energy every cycle. If power runs out, it fails.
- **Firing gives you away.** For about a cycle after you fire a phaser or launch a torpedo or probe, the enemy can see you. Being hit does the same.
- Damage to the cloaking device switches it off, and a damaged cloak can't be engaged until it is repaired.

## Hunting a cloaked ship

When an enemy that can cloak vanishes at close range, it has almost certainly cloaked, and it has not gone far. Watch for the moment it fires, and have tubes ready. Wide-spread phasers and torpedoes with a big proximity are your best chance.
