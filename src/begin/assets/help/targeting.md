Your target is the ship your weapons and the fire computer concentrate on. Click a ship, press **Tab**, or use `target`.

```console
target gorkon
target #7
target gorkon propulsion
target off
```

## Subsystems

Add a subsystem to aim at part of the target: **propulsion**, **weapons**, **shields**, **power**, **sensors** or **cloak**. Press **R** to cycle them. Aimed fire is a little less accurate, but each hit is far more likely to disable what you aimed at. Shots aimed at the hull spread damage at random.

> **Tip** Crippling the engines of a fast raider, or the weapons of a dreadnought, can matter more than raw damage.
