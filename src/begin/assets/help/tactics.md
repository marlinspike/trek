## Control the range

The side that chooses the range usually wins. Ships armed mainly with torpedoes want to stand off just inside their torpedo envelope, where enemy phasers cannot reach them. Ships with strong phasers want to close fast and stay close. Use `pursue <ship> warp <w>` to hold a chase, and `elude <ship>` to keep away. The [[coach]] tells you which side of each envelope you are on.

## Turn your strongest shield toward the enemy

Your ship has six shields, each covering 60°. Damage to one shield does not touch the others, so when the forward shield is weak, turn so a fresh one faces the threat. A shield can be **reinforced** to lose strength more slowly, at four times the energy cost. See [[shields]].

## Don't run hot

High warp heats your drives. A drive that climbs past its temperature limit burns out for good, and only a starbase can rebuild it. Fight at the warp you need, not the warp you have.

## Mix your weapons

Fire torpedoes from range, then finish with phasers when the target's shields are down. Keep a phaser bank or two on **defense** so that point defense can shoot down incoming torpedoes. See [[firecontrol]].

## Aim at what matters

A ship with no weapons cannot hurt you, and a ship with no engines cannot run. Use `target <ship> weapons` or `target <ship> propulsion` to aim at a subsystem. See [[targeting]].

## Use your allies

Allies follow standing orders. `tell <ally> attack <ship>` concentrates fire, `tell <ally> escort <you>` keeps a guard close, and `tell <ally> run` saves a damaged ship. See [[tell]].

## Know when to leave

A ship that stays more than 60,000 units from every enemy for ten cycles escapes the battle. A ship that escapes is not lost.
